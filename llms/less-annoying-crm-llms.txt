# Less Annoying CRM

> Simple, affordable CRM software built specifically for small businesses. Self-funded since 2009 with a commitment to keeping CRM easy to use and affordable.

## Product Information
- [Pricing](https://www.lessannoyingcrm.com/pricing): Simple $15/user/month pricing with no contracts, tiers, or hidden fees
- [Features](https://www.lessannoyingcrm.com/tour): Core CRM features including contact management, lead tracking, calendar, tasks, and form builder
- [Free Trial](https://www.lessannoyingcrm.com/help/free-trial): Complete 30-day free trial with full access to all features
- [Form builder](https://www.lessannoyingcrm.com/forms): Native form builder automatically adds and updates your CRM data.
- [Industries we serve](https://www.lessannoyingcrm.com/industry): List of the most common industries that use Less Annoying CRM
